Following a successful and informative workshop our team presented at the CHEDDAR Hub Summer School in Windsor, some of our team have been presenting at the [CHEDDAR Hub's Friends and Industry event](https://cheddarhub.org/) hosted in Central Hall Westminster, London.

![Jinkun presents 5G work](/img/posts/jinkun-presenting-cheddar.jpg)

The first day, hosted by Dr Poonam Yadav, featured demonstrations and presentations from across each of the CHEDDAR Hub's universities, and featuring a variety of project by some of CHEDDAR's partner universities. Our own Dr Jinkun Zhang presented some of [our work on xApp-driven RAN optimisation](/projects/xapp-driven-ran-optimisation) as part of a series of talks providing more detail about our demos, and Joshua Levett later gave a talk on our work to build visibility of the resilience of telecoms networks, [recently presented at ACM MobiSys 2026]((/publications/2026-visualising-mobile-operator-connectivity)).

On the second day, we were graced by a variety of industry experts participating in highly engaging panels on the future of mobile connectivity - whether AI-native of progress beyond 6G. We also had the opportunity to demonstrate some of our ongoing research.
