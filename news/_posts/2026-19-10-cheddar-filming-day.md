The CHEDDAR Hub visited the Systron Lab, to continue their filming project on capturing the people and research helping to shape the future of telecommunications.

This latest filming session featured Dr Poonam (Hiwal) Yadav sharing insights into her research and the work taking place at York as part of the CHEDDAR Telecoms Hub.

Researchers, Bidushi Barau, Mohit Bidikar, Jinkun Zhang, Dema Williams and Hasan Abo Dihin were also interviewed, giving insights into the meaningful work they have contributed to. 

Reserachers also demonstarted a number of projects for the filiming project, which included [xApp Driven RAN Optimisation for Mission Critical Robots](https://systronlab.github.io/projects/xapp-driven-ran-optimisation), [5G RedCap Support within OCUDU](https://systronlab.github.io/projects/redcap-interoperability) and [Live Network Traffic Analysis of IoT devices within the Thread Device Testbed](https://systronlab.github.io/projects/testbed-visualiser).
